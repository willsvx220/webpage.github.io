Trick #01 • Native Overlay

The Built-In <dialog> Modal

Press ESC to close automatically!

Browsers have a native <dialog> tag! When you call .showModal(), the browser automatically blurs the background using ::backdrop, traps your keyboard focus inside, and lets you close it with the Esc key!

🛸

Secret Mission Briefing

💡 Pro Tip: Notice how you can't accidentally click buttons behind the modal while it's open!

Under the Hood: modal.html 
<!-- 1. Define the hidden native dialog -->
<dialog id="myDialog">
  <h3>Hello Hugo! 👋</h3>
  <p>I am a native browser modal!</p>
  
  <!-- method="dialog" closes it with ZERO JS! -->
  <form method="dialog">
    <button>Close Mission</button>
  </form>
</dialog>

<!-- 2. Open it with one line -->
<button onclick="myDialog.showModal()">
  Open Modal
</button>

Trick #02 • Zero-JavaScript UI

Instant Accordions with <details> & <summary>

0 Lines of JavaScript

Click the secret drawers below! Bonus trick: because all three share the same name="hugo-accordion" attribute, opening one automatically closes the others!

🦖 Why is HTML called a "Markup" language?
HTML stands for HyperText Markup Language. Instead of doing math like Python or JavaScript, HTML wraps text in tags (like <h1> or <button>) to tell the browser what each piece of content is!
⚡ How does the exclusive accordion trick work?
Modern browsers added the name="..." attribute to <details>! Just like radio buttons, any <details> tags with the same name automatically team up so only one stays open at a time.
🏆 Secret Trophy Inside!
🎉 Boom! You found the hidden trophy without a single onClick listener!
Under the Hood: accordion.html 
<!-- Add "open" to start expanded -->
<details name="faq" open>
  <summary>Click me to toggle!</summary>
  <p>Hidden secret content revealed!</p>
</details>

<!-- Same name="faq" auto-closes the first! -->
<details name="faq">
  <summary>Second Drawer</summary>
  <p>Only one stays open at a time!</p>
</details>
Trick #03 • Brand-New Browser Superpower

The Native popover API

Light-Dismiss Built In

Want a floating tooltip, menu, or toast card that closes automatically when you click anywhere outside of it ("light-dismiss")? Just give any <div> the popover attribute and link a button with popovertarget!

🎈

Zero-JS Popover!

I float in the browser's Top Layer (above every z-index!). Click anywhere outside me or press Esc and I disappear automatically!

📌 Sticky Note (manual)

Because I use popover="manual", clicking outside won't close me—only my ✕ button can!

⚡ Cool Fact: Popovers render in the browser's Top Layer, so you never have to fight with z-index: 999999 ever again!

Under the Hood: popover.html 
<!-- Button points to the popover's ID -->
<button popovertarget="secret-tip">
  Toggle Popover
</button>

<!-- Add "popover" attribute to any box -->
<div id="secret-tip" popover>
  🎈 Click outside me to auto-close!
</div>

<!-- Or use popover="manual" to stay pinned -->
<div id="sticky" popover="manual">...</div>
